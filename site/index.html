Nematode

A little buddy who stomps on your Claude tab.

Download For Mac and Windows

Finds your tab

Open Claude in Chrome and he walks over and stomps on it, tabs on top or down the side.

Throw him around

Grab him, fling him, watch him bounce off your screen and stroll back.

Pick who and how

Nematode or Croissant, five app icons. Lives in your menu bar and updates himself.

Installing

Mac: open the download and drag Nematode into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the app: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Then click Allow so he can see your tabs.

Windows: run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

Five app icons. Soft Pink shows whoever's out. Switch from the menu bar.
